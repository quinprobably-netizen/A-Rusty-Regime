# Stage 1b: vehicles (done 2026-10-17)

Q asked for vehicles based on the Humvee, the Willys jeep and military dirt bikes, using Regolith
Rally's physics with bodies designed for this game.

## What was built
- `src/vehicle/physics.rs`: Regolith's tyre curve (`tyreForce`, TYRE_SURF gravel/tarmac), coil-over
  (`coilover` with bump/rebound knees, deep rebound, hydraulic and hard stops, SUSP constants),
  engine torque curve and engine braking (`engineTorque`, `engineNet`), ported to f32.
- `src/vehicle/spec.rs`: Humvee, jeep and dirt bike as numbers (mass, wheelbase, track, radius,
  travel, ride frequency, torque curve, gears, final drive, brakes, drag, seat, exit, camera).
- `src/vehicle/mod.rs`: `VehiclePlugin`. Each vehicle is an Avian dynamic box with raycast wheels
  stepped in FixedUpdate before the player controller: suspension load along the ground normal at
  the contact, tyre forces from relaxed slip ratio and angle (clamped so a tick never reverses the
  slip), wheel spin with drive and brake torque, automatic gearbox, reverse from a standstill,
  parking brake when driverless, air drag, kill-plane respawn to the spawn transform. Bikes set
  their roll rate toward the balanced lean each tick. `Driving(vehicle)` on the player disables
  its collider and the controller tick; the chase camera lives in `camera.rs`.
- `src/vehicle/body.rs`: bodies from scaled unit cubes and cylinders; `WheelVisual` children
  follow compression, steer and spin.
- Spawns in `island.rs`; `[vehicle]` tuning; E / gamepad X to enter; 5 new tests (34 total).

## Not done / later
- No rider model on the bike, no engine sound, no HUD gear or rpm.
- Vehicles use the gravel tyre curve everywhere; roads could switch to tarmac.
- Soldiers ignore vehicles (they still shoot the driver).
